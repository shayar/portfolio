// FOUNDING ENGINEER · 2025 – PRESENT

LinguaFlow AI

A production-grade multi-agent AI platform I designed and built as the sole engineer. Real-time event pipeline over Kafka: a hardware scanner captures input, flows through consumer groups, gets processed by LangChain agents with tool-calling, per-session memory, multi-step reasoning, and safety guardrails, then persisted to PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and Redis. The guardrail piece took the most iteration to get right — agents would produce responses that looked correct but were wrong in subtle ways. I built evaluation logic that compares outputs against expected schemas and quality thresholds before anything reaches a user. Full observability: OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana. Measurement is how you know whether a system is actually working, not just technically functional.

// FOUNDING ENGINEER · 2025 – PRESENT

Tell Me More

I built this because most LLM deployments lack the evaluation infrastructure needed to detect subtle reliability problems before they affect users. The platform routes identical prompts across OpenAI, Gemini, and Anthropic, logging response quality, latency, and token cost per run. The core research contribution is the evaluation schema — designed to support structured A/B comparisons across model versions and prompt templates, and to distinguish whether a change actually improved output quality or just shifted the failure mode.
